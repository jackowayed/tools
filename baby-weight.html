Sync between devices

Create a sync key here, then paste it on another device. Connecting combines this browser’s weigh-ins with that log; the synced birth date and sex take precedence.

Opening local storage…

Anyone with the key can read and change this log. Keep a copy: there is no key recovery. Sync stores readable records on the server over HTTPS; it is not end-to-end encrypted. Deleting a weigh-in hides it on all devices, but the server retains its history. Disconnect keeps this device’s data and pending changes.

Weigh-ins and settings are saved on this device. When you enable sync, weigh-ins, birth date and sex are also stored on the server. Unit preference stays on this device.
